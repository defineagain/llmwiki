# Bias Check

Stub — created to satisfy a `[[wikilink]]` from the seed pages.

The bias check is the [[llm-wikid]] schema's discipline for preventing
the wiki from becoming an echo chamber. Every concept, synthesis, and
source page must include:

- `## Counter-arguments` — what pushes back against the claims on this page.
- `## Data gaps` — what we don't know, what's missing.
- Inline `> [!contradiction]` callouts for conflicts within a page.
