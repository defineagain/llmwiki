# Obsidian

Stub — created to satisfy a `[[wikilink]]` from the seed pages.

Obsidian is a desktop knowledge-base application that reads a folder
of markdown files as a "vault." Wikilinks, graph view, dataview, and
Bases (1.9.10+) are built in. The shannhk/llm-wikid repo uses
Obsidian as its primary reader; the wiki is browsable in Obsidian
without modification.
